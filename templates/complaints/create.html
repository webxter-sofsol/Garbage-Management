What to Report
  • Overflowing or damaged bins

What Happens Next
  1. We receive and log your report
  2. An authority assigns a staff member

Tips for a Good Report
  • Be as specific as possible about the location
  • Add photos if you can — it speeds up resolution
  • Describe the severity (e.g. blocking traffic, health hazard)
  • Submit as soon as you notice the issue
