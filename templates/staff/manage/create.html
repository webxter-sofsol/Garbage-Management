What happens when you create a staff member?
  • A new user account is created with the provided email address.
  • The account is marked as a Staff Member (not a citizen).
  • A random secure password is generated — the staff member logs in using OTP via email.
  • A staff profile is created with a unique STF-XXXXXX ID.
  • The staff member can then receive complaint assignments and update their location.
Note

Make sure the email address is valid and accessible by the staff member. They will use it to receive OTP codes for login.
